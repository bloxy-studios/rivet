# ADR-XXXX: Title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-YYYY | Rejected
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** who was involved
- **Phase/rung:** which part of the plan forced this decision

## Context

What situation forces a decision? What constraints (technical, product, licensing,
operational) apply? Link evidence — benchmarks, docs, prior art — rather than asserting.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly and specifically enough that a reviewer can tell whether
code complies with it.

## Alternatives considered

For each realistic alternative: what it is, why it was not chosen. "We didn't consider
alternatives" is a reason to reject the ADR.

## Consequences

What becomes easier; what becomes harder; what we are explicitly accepting (costs,
risks, lock-ins); what would trigger revisiting this decision.
